How to play

Your room is buried under thousands of completely mixed-up crayons: 30 shades across 10 colors, all jumbled together. Each shade gets its own sorting box, and you start with boxes for only 6 of them.

  • Grab: click a crayon to pick it up. (The Bigger Handfuls upgrade lets you scoop up several of the same shade at once.)
  • Sort: click (or drag onto) the box with the matching shade. Every crayon earns coins.
  • Hover a crayon to see its shade name. Shades are sneaky!
  • Pack: when a box is full, click it to pack it up for a bonus. Packed boxes go on your shelves and earn coins over time.
  • Shop: buy boxes for the other shades, shelves, decorations (they make the room cozier, which boosts every coin you earn) and upgrades like bigger handfuls, shade highlighting and little Sortbots that sort for you.
  • Move: rearrange boxes and decorations however you like.

New piles get dumped in every so often. Progress saves automatically in this browser.
