Cray Sorting

This room is buried under 60,000 completely mixed-up crayons: 30 shades across 10 colors, covering every inch of the floor. Nobody sorted anything. Your job is to sort every last one.

WASD walk · Shift run
Mouse look around (click 🎯 Mouse look, or drag)
Click a crayon to pick it up · click a box to toss it in
Click a box with empty hands (or T) to take crayons back out
Click the floor to drop crayons · Q drop at your feet
Click a full box to pack it onto the nearest shelf · drawers stack, so build towers
E build (free boxes for any shade, shelves, furniture) · F collection
G move what you're looking at · R rotate · X put away · C change an empty box's shade

Each crayon only counts in the box or drawer for its exact shade. Look at one to see its name. Boxes, stackable drawers and shelves are free, so lay the room out however you like. Sorting enough of a color unlocks upgrades and furniture: open the Collection to see what's next. Progress saves in this browser.
